Australian Government AI transparency statements
This page lists Australian Government entities that have published AI transparency statements in line with the
Standard for AI transparency statements
under the
Policy for the responsible use of AI in government
(the AI Policy). These statements are intended to build public trust and provide a consistent basis for understanding and comparing how government agencies adopt AI.
Statements are published by individual agencies on their own websites. The Digital Transformation Agency (DTA) maintains the list of links on this page as a central reference.
This page was last updated on 23 June 2026. The DTA will periodically ensure that links are correct and update this page when entities advise of changes.
Agencies are responsible for the content, accuracy and currency of their own statements. The DTA does not validate or endorse individual statements.
For questions about this page, email
ai@dta.gov.au
. Questions about a specific agency's AI use should be directed to that agency.

Voluntary statements
The Policy does not apply to corporate Commonwealth entities (CCEs), the defence portfolio and the national intelligence community (NIC) as defined by section 4 of the
Office of National Intelligence Act 2018
.
These entities may choose to publish AI transparency statements on a voluntary basis. Defence portfolio agencies and the NIC may choose to publish transparency statements where this does not compromise national security capabilities or interests.
