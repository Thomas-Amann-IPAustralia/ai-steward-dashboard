About the AI impact assessment tool
The impact assessment tool is for Australian Government teams working on an artificial intelligence (AI) use case. It helps teams identify, assess and manage AI use case impacts and risks against Australia's AI Ethics Principles. Understanding and managing AI use case impacts and risks is critical for effective AI governance and to fulfilling the Australian Government’s commitment to safe and responsible use of AI. The impact assessment tool supports the
Policy for the responsible use of AI in government
.
How to use the tool
The Impact Assessment Tool is available as a fillable Word template that can be downloaded for use within your agency’s environment. Once downloaded, it can be seamlessly integrated into your existing workflows in undertaking consistent and structured assessments.
The template is accompanied by a detailed guidance document that corresponds to each numbered section of the tool. This guidance provides clear, practical advice for completing every part, helping you apply the tool effectively within your agency’s processes.

Testing the impact assessment tool
The DTA piloted an earlier draft of the impact assessment tool with 21 volunteer agencies from September to November 2024. Pilot participants provided valuable feedback that has informed further updates to the tool.
This earlier draft of the tool was known as the ‘Pilot AI assurance framework’. Since then, the title has been updated to ‘AI impact assessment tool’, to better reflect its intended scope and purpose.
