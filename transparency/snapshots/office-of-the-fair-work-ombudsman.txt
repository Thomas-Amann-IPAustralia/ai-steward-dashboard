AI transparency statement
We are committed to a safe and responsible use of Artificial Intelligence (AI). Its use has been informed by genuine staff consultation.
Read our AI transparency statement to learn more.

Our statement
Our AI transparency statement sets out how we:
- use AI
- govern and manage AI-related risks
- meet the Australian Government’s Policy for the Responsible Use of AI in Government 2.0.
We are committed to the safe and responsible use of AI to support our purpose of promoting harmonious, productive, cooperative, and compliant workplace relations.
This means that while AI systems and capabilities may provide insights or administrative support, all decisions regarding compliance, enforcement, and entitlements, are made by authorised staff.
AI systems and capabilities are strictly advisory, and their outputs are reviewed and verified by our staff.
